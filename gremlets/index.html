Gremlets

Tiny pixel creatures that live on your Mac desktop.

Sixty eight one-of-a-kind pixel creatures that walk along the Dock line, hop up onto your app windows, ride along when you drag a window, play tag, nap in piles and throw dance parties. They keep you company while you focus and hide when you are on a call.
