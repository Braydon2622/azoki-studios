Hearthside

A hand-painted village that rearranges itself around your icons.

A small hand-painted village seen from high above, living behind your desktop icons. Icons get open plazas under them, houses and trees step aside, paths link groups of icons, and villagers, a cat and a dog stroll the lanes. Drag an icon somewhere new and the ground ripples out from where you dropped it.
