Code Sea

A living ocean behind your desktop icons, drawn entirely in text.

Every creature is a 3D model rasterised into monospace characters, every frame. Jellyfish pulse and rise, a school of hundreds flows and flashes, sharks hunt, and a whale rises out of the dark and fills the screen.

02 live wallpaper

Hearthside

A hand-painted village that rearranges itself around your icons.

Icons get open plazas under them, houses and trees step aside, paths link groups of icons, and villagers, a cat and a dog stroll the lanes. Drag an icon and the ground ripples out from where you dropped it.

[ about ]

A wallpaper should be the best-behaved thing on your computer.

Azoki Studios is one person making things for the desktop. Every one of them follows the same three rules.

Know where the icons are

The village opens a plaza under each one. The fish swim round them. Nothing is ever drawn brighter than a label.
