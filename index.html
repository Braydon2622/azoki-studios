01 live wallpaper

Code Sea

A living ocean behind your desktop icons, drawn entirely in text.

Every creature is a 3D model rasterised into monospace characters, every frame. Jellyfish pulse and rise, a school of hundreds flows and flashes, sharks hunt, and a whale rises out of the dark and fills the screen.

03 live wallpaper

Hearthside

A hand-painted village that rearranges itself around your icons.

Icons get open plazas under them, houses and trees step aside, paths link groups of icons, and villagers, a cat and a dog stroll the lanes. Drag an icon and the ground ripples out from where you dropped it.

06 desktop companions

Gremlets

Tiny pixel creatures that live on your Mac desktop.

Sixty eight one-of-a-kind creatures that walk the Dock line, hop onto your windows, build staircases to the ones out of reach and nap in piles. They grow a bonsai for every project you work on, and hide when you are on a call.

07 desktop game

Winterville

A pixel village that spends the winter on your desktop.

A snowy town settles along the bottom of your screen. Snow piles up on your real windows, snow folk parachute in and ride your cursor, and one calendar door a day grows the town all the way to Christmas. Start a focus session and they build a snowman while you work.

[ about ]

A wallpaper should be the best-behaved thing on your computer.

Azoki Studios is one person making things for the desktop. Every one of them follows the same three rules.

  [ ]   [ ]
   \     /
 ~~~ ><> ~~~

Know where the icons are

The village opens a plaza under each one. The fish swim round them. Nothing is ever drawn brighter than a label.

Cost almost nothing

Hearthside takes 5 to 9% of one core while it moves and under 1% when a window covers it. Code Sea pauses itself the moment you start working.
